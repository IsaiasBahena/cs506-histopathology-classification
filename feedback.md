Project topic and timeline are clear, and using CRC-VAL-HE-7K as a held-out set from different patients is a nice touch. Your goals are clearly stated but not quite measurable yet. "How accurately can classical models classify..." doesn't tell us what counts as success. I'd suggest naming a baseline (eg majority class, or a simple color-histogram + logistic regression) and stating what improvement you're aiming for. For the clustering question, name the metric you'll use to compare clusters to labels, such as adjusted Rand index or NMI. One practical issue: running SVD on raw pixels for 100,000 images at 224x224x3 is roughly a 100K x 150K matrix, which won't fit in memory. You'll likely need to downsample the images (eg to 32x32 or 64x64), use a subsample, or use randomized or incremental SVD, so plan for that in weeks 3-4. Also be specific about which handcrafted features you'll use (color histograms, GLCM, LBP, etc.)
